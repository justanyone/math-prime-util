- `n.a.`: a `BigUint` form does not apply; the reason says why.

## Native-only names

Each group gives the value, the reason, and the names. `n.a.` groups come first.

### integer operators

`n.a.`, 26 names. Plain integer arithmetic, comparison, shifts and parity on `i128`. num-bigint's operators (`+ - * / % << >>`, `pow`, comparison) and the num-integer `Integer` trait it implements (`div_floor`, `mod_floor`, `div_rem`, `div_ceil`, `is_even`, `is_odd`, `is_multiple_of`) do the same on `BigUint` and `BigInt`; a wrapper adds nothing.

`absint`, `add1int`, `addint`, `cdivint`, `cdivrem`, `cmpint`, `divint`, `divrem`, `fdivrem`, `is_congruent`, `is_divisible`, `is_even`, `is_odd`, `lshiftint`, `modint`, `muladdint`, `mulint`, `mulsubint`, `negint`, `powint`, `rashiftint`, `rshiftint`, `signint`, `sub1int`, `subint`, `tdivrem`
